# 🔄 Reflection – Sprint Backlog

## ⭐ STAR Method Reflection

---

### ⭐ Situation

During the development of PowerShare, a portable power station rental platform, the team needed to organise the work required to develop and improve the system. The sprint backlog helped break the project work into smaller tasks that could be tracked using Trello.

---

### 🎯 Task

As the Full-Stack Developer, my responsibility was to contribute to the technical development of PowerShare and organise development activities into manageable tasks. These tasks needed to support the implementation of the platform's features, including customer authentication, battery information, rental bookings, and communication between the frontend and backend.

---

### ⚙️ Action

I used the sprint backlog structure to organise development activities into smaller tasks and monitor their progress on Trello. I focused on the technical requirements of the application and considered how individual tasks contributed to the overall project goals. I also recognised the importance of keeping tasks understandable and ensuring that progress was recorded clearly so the team could follow the work being completed.

---

### ✅ Result

The sprint backlog helped me understand how larger development goals can be divided into smaller, manageable activities. It also highlighted the importance of task tracking, prioritisation, and communication in Agile development. One challenge was ensuring that the recorded progress accurately reflected the actual work completed. In future sprints, I would improve the process by agreeing on sprint goals with the team, defining clear completion criteria, updating Trello consistently, and supporting completed tasks with evidence such as screenshots, tests, and GitHub commits.

---

### 💡 Reflection and Learning

This activity improved my understanding of how sprint backlogs support planning and accountability. I learned that completing software features is important, but maintaining accurate task records is also necessary to demonstrate progress. I would apply this lesson in future projects by keeping the sprint backlog updated throughout development and reviewing completed tasks with the team.
