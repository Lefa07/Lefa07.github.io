**Target Market:** Micro-entrepreneurs and informal traders in township areas of Cape Town, South Africa.

---

## 1. Team Purpose

### 1.1 Project Objective

The primary objective of the Power-Share project is to develop a sustainable business solution that assists micro-entrepreneurs and informal traders in township areas such as Khayelitsha and Mitchells Plain in Cape Town to continue operating during load shedding and power outages.

Many small businesses rely heavily on electricity to operate essential equipment such as hair clippers, sewing machines, internet routers, lights and card machines. However, many township entrepreneurs cannot afford expensive backup power solutions such as generators, solar installations or lithium battery systems. As a result, these businesses may experience operational interruptions and financial losses during power outages.

Power-Share proposes a portable power station rental service that allows small businesses to rent pre-charged lithium power stations at an affordable daily rate of R50–R80. This solution aims to help businesses maintain their operations during outages without requiring a large upfront investment.

The project team aims to:

* Research the impact of load shedding on township businesses.
* Design a feasible rental-based backup power solution.
* Develop a marketing strategy targeting micro-entrepreneurs.
* Present a practical business model that addresses real community challenges.
* Develop a digital platform to support the Power-Share rental service.
* Provide a convenient way for customers to interact with the service and manage rental requests.

### 1.2 Vision Statement

To create an affordable and accessible power rental solution that empowers township entrepreneurs to remain productive and financially stable despite electricity disruptions.

### 1.3 Project Mission

The mission of Power-Share is to improve access to backup electricity by offering an affordable rental alternative to purchasing expensive power equipment.

The project combines a community-focused business concept with a digital platform intended to support customer access, rental bookings and administrative management.

### 1.4 Project Success Criteria

The project aims to demonstrate success through:

* A clear and practical business concept.
* Research into the needs of township entrepreneurs.
* A marketing strategy aimed at the intended target market.
* A well-structured business model.
* A functional digital platform supporting relevant rental activities.
* Clear project documentation and presentation materials.
* Effective collaboration and communication among team members.

The team should evaluate these criteria against the actual project requirements and deliverables.

## 3. Team Values and Principles

### 3.1 Core Team Values

#### Collaboration

Team members should work cooperatively, share ideas openly and support one another in completing tasks and solving problems.

Collaboration is particularly important when connecting software functionality, interface design and documentation.

#### Accountability

Each team member is responsible for completing assigned tasks and communicating when difficulties affect progress.

Accountability helps the team understand what has been completed, what remains outstanding and where support may be required.

#### Transparency

Progress, challenges, changes and relevant decisions should be communicated openly.

Transparent communication helps prevent misunderstandings and allows team members to respond to problems earlier.

#### Respect

Team members should treat one another professionally and respectfully while recognising different perspectives, skills and contributions.

Respectful collaboration creates an environment where members can share ideas and raise concerns constructively.

#### Continuous Improvement

The team should identify opportunities to improve its working methods, technical implementation, documentation and final deliverables.

Feedback should be used to improve the project rather than simply identify mistakes.

### 3.2 Agile Principles

#### Teamwork and Collaboration

Agile encourages active participation and shared responsibility. Team members should communicate regularly and coordinate work that depends on other tasks.

#### Adaptability

The team should be prepared to adjust its approach when new requirements, technical problems or research findings arise.

Changes should be discussed and recorded where appropriate.

#### Delivering Value to Users

The project should remain focused on addressing the challenges faced by township entrepreneurs affected by electricity interruptions.

Technical decisions and proposed features should support the intended users' needs.

#### Continuous Feedback

Feedback from team members, project reviews and available user research can help identify areas for improvement.

The team should use relevant feedback to refine its work.

#### Incremental Development

Where applicable, the digital platform should be developed and tested in manageable stages. This allows the team to identify problems and evaluate functionality as development progresses.

#### Clear Communication

Team members should communicate expectations, task progress and challenges so that everyone understands the current state of the project.

## 8. Managing Challenges and Changes

Project work may involve technical difficulties, changing requirements, time constraints or coordination challenges.

When a challenge arises, the team should:

1. Identify and describe the problem.
2. Communicate the issue to the relevant team members.
3. Determine its effect on project progress.
4. Discuss possible solutions.
5. Agree on the appropriate next steps.
6. Update relevant tasks or documentation.
7. Review whether the solution addressed the problem.

For technical issues, the Full-Stack Developer may investigate implementation or integration problems. Design-related issues can be discussed with the UI/UX Designer, while documentation issues can be reviewed with the Documentation and Editor role.

Where an issue affects multiple areas, the team should work together to find a suitable solution.
