# Reflection 1: Agile Team Charter

## Situation

The PowerShare project aims to provide an affordable portable power station rental solution for small businesses and informal traders affected by load shedding in South Africa. Our team consists of three members: myself, Mojalefa Mabotja, as the Full-Stack Developer; Magdeline Malahlela, responsible for UI/UX design; and Refilwe Mabena, responsible for documentation and editing.

We use WhatsApp for communication and Trello to organise project tasks. GitHub supports our source-code collaboration, while Figma is used for interface design.

## Task

My responsibility was to contribute to the development of the PowerShare platform while working with the team to establish clear responsibilities and effective collaboration practices.

The Agile Team Charter helps document the project's purpose, team roles, communication methods, values and goals. It provides a shared reference for understanding how the team intends to work together.

## Action

I documented the team's responsibilities and collaboration arrangements. As the Full-Stack Developer, I focused on the technical aspects of PowerShare, including frontend and backend development, system integration, troubleshooting and testing.

I also considered how the team's communication and task management tools could support collaboration. WhatsApp provides a channel for team discussions, Trello helps organise tasks, GitHub supports code collaboration, and Figma supports interface design.

## Result

The revised charter provides a clearer description of the three team members and their responsibilities. It also documents the project's purpose, communication arrangements, values and milestones.

Having these responsibilities documented helps establish expectations and provides a reference for coordinating work. The charter can also be reviewed when responsibilities change or the team identifies areas that require improvement.

## Reflection and Learning

Preparing the Agile Team Charter helped me understand that successful software development requires more than technical skills. Clear responsibilities, communication, accountability and cooperation are also important for achieving project objectives.

As a Full-Stack Developer, I recognise the importance of coordinating frontend and backend work with interface design and documentation. Technical decisions can affect other areas of the project, so communication with the rest of the team is essential.

In future projects, I would establish a team charter at the beginning of development and ensure that all members review and agree on their responsibilities. I would also encourage regular progress updates and consistent task tracking to identify problems early and improve teamwork.
