# 🤝 Reflection – Agile Team Charter

## ⭐ STAR Method Reflection

---

### ⭐ Situation

The PowerShare project aims to provide an affordable portable power station rental solution for small businesses and informal traders affected by load shedding in South Africa.

Our team consists of three members: myself, Mojalefa Mabotja, as the Full-Stack Developer; Magdeline Malahlela, responsible for UI/UX design; and Refilwe Mabena, responsible for documentation and editing.

We use WhatsApp for communication and Trello to organise project tasks. GitHub supports our source-code collaboration, while Figma is used for interface design.

---

### 🎯 Task

My responsibility was to contribute to the development of the PowerShare platform while working with the team to establish clear responsibilities and effective collaboration practices.

The Agile Team Charter documents the project's purpose, team roles, communication methods, values, and goals. It provides a shared reference for understanding how the team intends to work together.

---

### ⚙️ Action

I documented the team's responsibilities and collaboration arrangements. As the Full-Stack Developer, I focused on the technical aspects of PowerShare, including frontend and backend development, system integration, troubleshooting, and testing.

I also considered how the team's communication and task management tools could support collaboration:

* **WhatsApp:** Provides a channel for team discussions and communication.
* **Trello:** Helps organise and track project tasks.
* **GitHub:** Supports source-code collaboration and version control.
* **Figma:** Supports interface design and visual planning.

These tools help the team coordinate different responsibilities and contribute towards the same project objectives.

---

### ✅ Result

The revised charter provides a clearer description of the three team members and their responsibilities. It also documents the project's purpose, communication arrangements, values, and milestones.

Having these responsibilities documented helps establish expectations and provides a reference for coordinating work. The charter can also be reviewed when responsibilities change or the team identifies areas that require improvement.

---

### 💡 Reflection and Learning

Preparing the Agile Team Charter helped me understand that successful software development requires more than technical skills. Clear responsibilities, communication, accountability, and cooperation are also important for achieving project objectives.

As a Full-Stack Developer, I recognise the importance of coordinating frontend and backend work with interface design
