# Reflection 4: Sprint Backlog

## Situation

PowerShare involves multiple development activities, including frontend development, backend development, interface design, integration and testing. These activities need to be organised so that the team can coordinate its work.

## Task

The purpose of a sprint backlog is to identify the work selected for a particular sprint and organise it into manageable tasks that contribute towards a defined sprint goal.

## Action

I prepared a proposed sprint backlog containing development activities related to authentication, battery browsing, rental bookings, payments, notifications, administrative functionality and testing.

I also considered the importance of task ownership, progress tracking and regular reviews. Trello can support this process by allowing the team to organise tasks and monitor their status.

## Result

The proposed document provides a framework for describing sprint goals, selected tasks, responsibilities, testing activities and review outcomes.

The draft still needs to be aligned with an actual sprint completed by the team. Its tasks, dates, assignments and statuses should be verified using Trello and other available project records.

## Reflection and Learning

I learned that breaking development work into smaller tasks can make a project easier to manage and its progress easier to evaluate. A clear sprint goal also helps the team understand what it is trying to achieve during a development cycle.

In future projects, I would establish a clear sprint goal, estimate the effort required for tasks and review progress regularly. I would also document unfinished work and discuss obstacles during the sprint review so that improvements can be made in subsequent sprints.
